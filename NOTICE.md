# Notice — exercise animation media

The looping exercise animations in `assets/vital/*.mp4` are from the
**Vital Animations Free Pack** — a free starter pack of HD 1080p gym-exercise
loops with structured JSON metadata (same schema as the full Vital dataset,
https://vital.com / Vital 3D exercise library). The pack is distributed as a
free "build-ready starter pack".

The bundled MP4s are the pack's original 1080p files, copied unmodified. The
clips are shot on a light studio background and are shown on a matching light
card in the app so the full frame is visible.

For any exercise without a Vital clip, the app automatically falls back to
its built-in animated SVG figure.

If you ship PULSE commercially, confirm the Vital Animations license terms
for the Free Pack / full dataset before distributing.
