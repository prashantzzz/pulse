NextRep — Account & Data Deletion

NextRep: Gym Workout Tracker • Developer: NextRep

NextRep gives you complete ownership and control over your personal fitness and account data. You can delete your data at any time directly in the app or submit a deletion request via this page.

1. In-App Instant Data Deletion

If you have the NextRep app installed, you can wipe all your workout records and local cache instantly:

  1. Open NextRep on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab → tap Settings (gear icon).
  3. Scroll to the Your Data section.
  4. Tap Clear all data and confirm.

All workouts, sets, exercise records, body measurements, and local session caches are immediately and permanently erased from your device.

2. Cloud Account & Associated Data Deletion Request

If you created a cloud account (via Email or Google OAuth) and wish to permanently delete your account, authentication credentials, and all synchronized cloud backups:

Step-by-Step Request Instructions:

  1. Send an email to support@nextrep.fit or prashant.coder.dev@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request - NextRep.
  3. Include the email address associated with your NextRep cloud account.

Our team verifies account ownership and processes complete permanent deletion within 7 business days.

3. Types of Data Deleted vs. Retained

Data Types Permanently Deleted:

  • Account Authentication: User ID, email address, display name, and hashed auth records.
  • Workout History: Logged workouts, exercises, set numbers, weights, reps, RPE, and notes.
  • Body Measurements: Weight records, body fat percentages, and body dimension logs.
  • Social & Challenge Data: Friend connections, active challenges, and leaderboard entries.
  • Storage: Custom avatar photos uploaded to our cloud storage.

Data Retained:

  • None: We do not retain any residual personal data or fitness records once account deletion is executed.

Data Retention Period:

  • Immediate for local data: Cleared on-device instantly upon clicking "Clear all data".
  • Within 7 days for cloud accounts: Full database record purging, storage cleanup, and authentication profile deletion upon receipt of your deletion request.
